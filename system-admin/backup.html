Restore a backup

  • Click Restore on a saved backup. For a backup you downloaded earlier, use Upload a backup file first.
  • All data and uploaded files go back to how they were when that backup was made. Anything added or changed later is lost.
  • A Safety backup is made first. To undo a restore, restore that one.
  • Everyone is signed out, including you, and signs in again afterwards.

Try one restore before you rely on your backups.

If the system will not open at all, the step-by-step guide is in RESTORE.txt inside each downloaded backup. Give it to whoever manages your server.
